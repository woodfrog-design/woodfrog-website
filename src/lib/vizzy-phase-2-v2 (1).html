Onboarding 12 / 19
Onboarding & Setup

Four steps from
dataset to production.

01 · CONNECT

Connect & discover

Connect the data source. The system automatically reads tables, columns, data types, and samples values to build an initial metadata profile.

Manual: confirm access and permissions.
02 · CLARIFY

Clarify & contextualize

The system analyzes schema and values, generates column meaning suggestions, and asks targeted questions only where business meaning is unclear.

Manual: confirm meanings and provide key business rules if needed.
03 · VALIDATE

Validate & tune

Run real business questions. The system generates, validates, and auto-fixes queries while learning patterns.

Manual: review outputs and confirm correctness.
04 · DEPLOY

Deploy & enable

Enable access for users with sample questions and guidance. System is ready for production use.

Manual: user onboarding and access control.

Limits 17 / 19
Honest Limits

Where Vizzy needs
a little help.

LIMIT 01

Ambiguous business terms

When a column could mean two things, Vizzy will ask rather than guess.

LIMIT 02

Poorly structured data

Inconsistent schemas and orphaned fields reduce accuracy.

LIMIT 03

Domain logic onboarding

Complex business rules need to be captured during setup.

LIMIT 04

Not a replacement for deep analysis

Vizzy speeds up analysts — it doesn't eliminate them.

These aren't blockers — they're guardrails. Vizzy is built to be upfront when it needs more context.
